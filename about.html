OUR ROLE IN THE COMMUNITY

Close to home, useful every day.

Uthram Stores is a neighbourhood convenience store at Meghamalhar Complex, Akkarapuram, Thrissur. The store serves everyday household shopping needs through a practical selection of groceries, dairy, fresh produce, snacks, beverages, personal-care products and cleaning supplies.

This website is designed to help nearby residents find the store, understand what categories are available and contact the shop quickly when they need a product.
